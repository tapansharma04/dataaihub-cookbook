# Knowledge Platform

Service documentation for the Knowledge Platform.

The Knowledge Platform indexes internal documents and serves retrieval
queries for Acme applications. Clients read this resource for current
service facts; they do not invent the catalog locally.

## Services

- knowledge-platform
- billing-api
- identity-api

## Capabilities

- Document ingest and chunking
- Keyword and vector retrieval
- Citation-preserving answer assembly

## Operational notes

Status and incident details live in `acme://status/services`.
Billing product surfaces live in `acme://docs/billing-portal`.
